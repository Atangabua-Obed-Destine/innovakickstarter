Squad roles. Every member has at least one; small squads combine them.
- **Facilitator** (changes each sprint): runs the daily check-in, keeps the board true, books the sprint review with the mentor.
- **Release lead**: owns CI and deployment.
- **Contract keeper**: owns `contract/README.md`, and makes sure every contract change is approved by each part that uses it.
- **Part owner** (API, web shop, rep app): plans the work in that part and reviews its pull requests.
